A notation is a promise that a certain amount of thinking has already been done. When I write $\mathbb{R}^n$ instead of "the set of ordered $n$-tuples of real numbers", I am not saving ink. I am telling the reader which facts I intend to take for granted.

The trouble begins when the promise is never made explicit.
